El Nota (النوتة)


El-Nota is Note-Planning / List-Making Web App. El-Nota helps in creating and managing your daily and schedule tasks and plans. With Friendly Displaying to your plans to increase your productivity.

النوتة هو تطبيق لتنظيم ملحظاتك و خطتك المستقبلية. النوتة يساعدك فى انشاء و تحكم فى ملحظاتك و خطتك اليومية و المنتظمة. بمساعدة النوتة يمكنك زيادة الانتاجية و الفاعليها فى حياتك اليومية

Product features:
  • Create and Manage Your Own To-Do Lists
  • Manage Your Daily Tasks and Your Schedule Plans
  • Extremely Friendly Display for your Tasks
  • Mark The Finished Tasks in Your To-Do Lists

Social Inviter: Simplify Your Connections


Social Inviter is your go-to app for effortlessly sharing your social and professional profiles. With Social Inviter, you can convert any URL link into a QR code, making it easy for others to scan and access your website or social media accounts instantly. No more exchanging emails or phone numbers—just scan and connect!

Key Features::
  • Instant QR Code Generation: Convert your social or professional URLs into QR codes with a single tap.
  • Seamless Sharing: Share your QR codes directly from the app via email, messaging apps, or social media.
  • Secure and Private: Your data is safe with us. Social Inviter ensures that your information is protected and only shared with those you choose.
  • User-Friendly Interface: Enjoy a clean, intuitive design that makes QR code generation and sharing a breeze.
